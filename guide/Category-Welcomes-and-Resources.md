# Category welcomes and Resources navigation

The Guide introduction and five category welcomes use Chase (United States), Simba 3.2, SSML +30%. Each category opens with its welcome and an unselected lesson dropdown. Get Started retains its existing introduction and autoplay. Selecting a Resource topic plays the recommendation and opens its verified primary YouTube destination after the narration finishes. A blocked new tab falls back to the current tab; Stop or a new selection cancels pending navigation. More displays only that topic’s resource links.

## Welcome to Fundamentals

Audio: `welcome-fundamentals.mp3`

Welcome to Fundamentals. Explore aiming, cue ball control, and the basics of a dependable stroke. Open the drop-down menu and choose a topic you want to practice. Listen to the lesson and follow along at your own pace.

## Welcome to Physics and Tools

Audio: `welcome-physics.mp3`

Welcome to Physics and Tools. Explore how speed, spin, and contact affect a shot, and learn to use the table’s visual tools. Choose a topic from the drop-down menu, then try the idea on the table and compare the result.

## Welcome to Drills

Audio: `welcome-drills.mp3`

Welcome to Drills. Choose a drill from the drop-down menu and listen to its setup instructions. Place the balls and markers, then work through the shots at your own pace. Repeat the same arrangement to build control and compare your results.

## Welcome to Exercises

Audio: `welcome-exercises.mp3`

Welcome to Exercises. Explore short challenges that connect aiming, cue ball control, and planning your next shot. Choose an exercise from the drop-down menu, listen to its instructions, and work through it on the table. Repeat it whenever you want to sharpen that skill.

## Welcome to Resources

Audio: `welcome-more.mp3`

We’ve found Tor Lowry’s Zero X Billiards lessons helpful in advancing our games. Welcome to Resources. Explore his training videos and websites. Choose a topic from the drop-down menu to hear its introduction. When the audio finishes, the matching website will open. Use More for the links to that resource.

## Welcome to the Guide

Audio: `welcome-guide.mp3`

Welcome to the Guide. Use Play and Pause to listen at your own pace. Turn on Auto to play through the lessons in your selected category without choosing each one. Tap Read to follow along with the narration. Reading while you listen can help you get more from each lesson. Open More for additional resources and links related to that topic. Explore Fundamentals, Physics and Tools, Drills, Exercises, and Resources. Tap any category, then choose a topic from its drop-down menu. Pick something that interests you, listen, and try it on the table.

The Guide launcher plays this introduction on every open, from zero, with continuous Auto off. Enabling Auto during a welcome starts the first eligible lesson when the welcome finishes and continues through the selected category. Category buttons retain their dedicated welcomes.
