# Additional and replacement audio recordings

These 21 scripts are ready for review and recording in the existing narration voice. No new MP3s have been generated. Keep the filenames below when exporting the recordings. The JSON queue records each control and its current mapping. Existing recordings remain in use until replacements are recorded and verified; the AI explanation currently uses device speech.

## 1. Image / Camera heading

Filename: `photo-image-camera-section-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: IMAGE / CAMERA heading

This section is where you choose the photograph you want to analyze. Select Choose Image to load a picture of the table. For a new camera photo, return to the pool-table screen and use the camera button beside Image Camera. Include the whole playing surface and use a clear, evenly lit image.

## 2. Table Setup heading

Filename: `photo-table-setup-section-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: TABLE SETUP heading

Table Setup turns your photograph into a usable ball layout. Start with Auto Corners and check the table alignment. Choose the rack type, then select Analyze Balls. Review the detected balls, add any missed balls, and correct their identities before applying the layout.

## 3. Reset Corners

Filename: `photo-reset-corners-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoResetCorners

Reset Corners clears the table reference points and detected balls so you can align the photograph again. Move the crosshair to each inside playing-surface corner and click in order: top left, top right, bottom right, then bottom left. Analyze the balls again after the alignment is correct.

## 4. AI detection mode

Filename: `photo-ai-mode-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoAiModeBtn

The AI button enables or disables the trained ball detector. Ready means the models are loaded. Auto or Loading means they are preparing. Retry restarts a failed load. With AI off, Analyze Balls uses the original detector. Review the detected identities and positions before applying your layout.

## 5. Saved Images dropdown

Filename: `photo-saved-image-select-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoArchiveSelect and SAVED IMAGES heading

Saved Images lists the photographs saved inside this app on this device. Choose the photograph you want to work with, then select Open. Choosing an entry in the list does not open it by itself.

## 6. Open saved image

Filename: `photo-open-saved-image-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoArchiveLoad

Open loads the photograph selected in Saved Images into the analysis workspace. Check the table corners and analyze the balls again. Saved Images stores the photograph; a saved trainer layout is kept separately.

## 7. Remove saved image

Filename: `photo-remove-saved-image-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoArchiveDelete

Remove deletes the selected photograph from the app’s saved images on this device. The button changes to Confirm Remove. Press it again to complete the removal. Copies you already downloaded remain on your device.

## 8. Save current image

Filename: `photo-save-image-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoArchiveSave

Save keeps the current photograph inside the app on this device so you can open it again from Saved Images. The button shows Saved when the image is already stored. To save the reviewed ball arrangement as a trainer layout, use Apply and Save Layout instead.

## 9. Download current image

Filename: `photo-download-image-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoArchiveExport

Download saves a JPEG copy of the current source photograph to your device. This gives you a separate image file you can keep or share. It does not save the detected ball arrangement as a trainer layout.

## 10. Layout name

Filename: `photo-layout-name-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: photoLayoutName

Enter a descriptive name for the reviewed ball arrangement, then choose Apply and Save Layout. Use a name that helps you recognize the practice situation later, such as a bank-shot setup or a three-ball runout.

## 11. Explorer launcher

Filename: `explorer-mode-quick.mp3`  
Control: Both pages: Explorer circle

Explorer is your tour of the trainer’s controls and layout. Turn it on, point to a button, field, ball, or visual reference, then click or tap to hear its explanation. While Explorer is on, those actions explain the feature without changing your setup. Turn Explorer off to use the controls normally.

## 12. Guide launcher

Filename: `guide-lessons-mode-quick.mp3`  
Control: Both pages: Guide circle

Guide contains techniques, practice ideas, drills, and exercises. Choose a category and lesson, then press Play. Read shows the lesson’s narration. The trainer controls remain available while you study. For a tour of what each control does, use the separate Explorer button.

## 13. Get Started launcher

Filename: `get-started-launcher-quick.mp3`  
Control: Pool page: Get Started circle

Get Started opens the introductory lesson list. Begin with the first lesson or choose the topic you need. The lessons introduce your first aiming adjustments, shot execution, and practice sequence. Use the A button if you want the next lesson to play automatically.

## 14. Autoplay A

Filename: `guide-autoplay-quick.mp3`  
Control: Both Guide players: A button

The A button turns autoplay on or off. Green means on, and gray means off. With autoplay on, the next lesson in the current category and skill-level list starts after the current lesson finishes. Playback stops at the end of that list. Select a lesson and press Play to begin.

## 15. Close Analyze

Filename: `photo-close-analysis-quick.mp3`  
Control: Analyze: Close X

The X closes the analysis workspace and returns you to the pool-table screen. To transfer your reviewed ball arrangement before leaving, select Apply to Trainer Table. Use Apply and Save Layout if you also want to keep that arrangement for future practice.

## 16. Learning at your own pace

Filename: `guide-learning-pace-v2.mp3`  
Control: Get Started: replace guide-learning-pace-quick

You do not need to complete every lesson before using the trainer. Use Explorer when you want to learn a control. Open Guide for techniques and practice ideas. Experiment with shots and return to any lesson whenever it helps.

## 17. Understanding controls

Filename: `guide-control-help-v2.mp3`  
Control: Get Started: replace guide-control-help-quick

When you are unsure what a control does, turn on Explorer. Point to the control and click or tap to hear its explanation. Turn Explorer off when you are ready to adjust the control and try it yourself.

## 18. Understanding visual references

Filename: `guide-visual-help-v2.mp3`  
Control: Get Started: replace guide-visual-help-quick

The trainer displays visual references for aiming, ball contact, and cue-ball movement. Turn on Explorer and click a reference to learn what it represents. Use Guide for lessons that show how to apply those ideas in practice.

## 19. Help for a specific feature

Filename: `guide-context-help-v2.mp3`  
Control: Get Started: replace guide-context-help-quick

Explorer explains the feature you click, whether it is a shot-speed control, tangent line, or layout tool. Guide provides the related techniques, drills, and exercises. Choose the tool that matches what you want to learn.

## 20. Skill level

Filename: `guide-learning-level-v2.mp3`  
Control: Both players: skill-level slider; replace guide-learning-level-quick

The skill-level slider changes which lessons are available. Begin at Beginner for the introductory explanations. Move to Intermediate or Advanced to include more detailed lessons. Choose a lesson from the updated list and study it at your own pace.

## 21. Playback controls

Filename: `guide-playback-controls-v2.mp3`  
Control: Both players: Stop, Play/Pause, Back, Forward; replace guide-playback-controls-quick

Play starts the selected lesson, and Pause holds your place. Stop returns the lesson to its beginning. Back and Forward move ten seconds through the recording. The A button turns automatic playback of the next lesson on or off. Read displays the narration text.
