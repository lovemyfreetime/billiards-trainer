All audio uses existing recordings with matching transcripts. Duplicate welcome/help/learning-pace messages and obsolete Quick Help / Advanced version selection instructions are removed from this menu and autoplay. The regular Guide curriculum and Explorer mappings remain available.

Starting layouts stay attached through retained first-session topic identities. Get Started keeps Auto on by default, begins at chapter one, and stops at the last chapter.
