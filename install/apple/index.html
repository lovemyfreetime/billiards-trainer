Safari Home Screen installation

Install on iPhone or iPad

Use Safari for the installation. Older iOS versions may not offer Add to Home Screen from Chrome.
  1. Tap Open Billiards Trainer below.
  2. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then launch Billiards Trainer from its Home Screen icon.
  5. After the first successful launch, the cached trainer can operate offline.
